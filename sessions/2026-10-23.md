## What we decided  (details go in decisions.md)
- Node.js, with Slack in Socket Mode, running on the Mac for now.
- Events are stored as a multi-select "Events" field on People.
- Emails and phones are added, never replaced. Name, title and company are overwritten, with old values logged first.
- Card reading uses Claude Opus 5.5 with automatic fallback.
- Local memory in `data/state.json` stops a post being processed twice and a note being added twice.

## What we learned
- The chat connections (Attio, Slack) only work inside the chat. The scanner needs its own keys in `.env`.
- The Attio chat connection can't create fields. It only creates records, notes, tasks, comments and lists.
